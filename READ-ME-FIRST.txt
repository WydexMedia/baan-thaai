BAAN THAI WEBSITE — what's in this folder
==========================================

index.html ............ The public booking page guests see.
staff/ ................ The staff login page (your-website/staff).
config.js ............. The ONE file you edit: paste your Google web app URL here.
assets/ ............... Logo, pictures, styles and the page code. Don't change.
robots.txt ............ Tells Google not to show the staff page in search.
google-apps-script/
   Code.gs ............ Copy this into your Google Sheet (Extensions > Apps Script).
                        It is NOT uploaded to the website.

Follow the setup guide step by step. You only need to edit config.js.
